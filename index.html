Why early-stage consumer

The asset class nobody has to hype.

Big CPG companies have to buy their growth, and they buy it from brands like ours. Consumer moves on a different cycle than tech, holds up when the economy does not, and rewards investors who know the category.

14.4

Acquisitions a year

The 15 largest CPG companies have averaged 14.4 acquisitions a year since 2000. The buyers are always shopping.

<$50M

Raised before the big exit

Siete, RXBAR and Liquid I.V. each reached nine-figure exits on less than $50M of outside capital. Wholesale growth burns less cash.

Through recessions

Consumer staples is the only S&P 500 sector with a positive average return during recessions. People still buy groceries.

About Springdale

Started by people who have done the job.

We partner with founders building consumer brands that change their categories, and we stay with them from the first check to the exit.

Our story
2019

Gen and Dan found Springdale Ventures. Fund I, $27M, backs 31 brands including Caraway, Goodles, and Beatbox.

2022

Fund II, $38M, backs 29 brands including Elm Biosciences, Arterra, and Magnolia Table.

2026

Three exits: Goodles is acquired by Barilla, Anheuser-Busch takes a majority stake in Beatbox, and Paine Schwartz Partners makes a majority investment in First Day.

2026

Fund III kicks off, backing brands like Glimmr and Magna.

Consumer founders deserve investors who have shipped a pallet.

Gen has spent twenty years building consumer brands and has founded two of her own. She was Managing Director of SKU, the Austin incubator behind Epic and Siete, which later sold to General Mills and PepsiCo. She co-created Naturally Network, the national community for natural products founders, and sits on the boards of Goodles and Magnolia Table.

Dan built BuildASign.com from a startup into a $100M-plus ecommerce business and sold it to Cimpress, Vistaprint's parent. He knows direct-to-consumer, operational scale, and what it takes to negotiate a profitable exit, because he has done all three with his own company.

At SKU, they both kept watching good brands struggle to raise from investors who had never sold a product. In 2019 they started a fund that invests the way an operator would. We look at velocity before vanity metrics. We ask about margin structure in the first meeting. And when a founder calls with a problem, there is a decent chance one of us has had that exact problem.

Seven years later we have backed more than 60 brands from a team of fewer than five people spread across Santa Fe, Austin, and Chicago. Over 60% of our companies are led by women or diverse founders, across 13 states.

This policy explains what information Springdale Ventures, LLC ("Springdale," "we," "us") collects through springdaleventures.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services that power parts of this site may receive basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit. We do not use advertising cookies or sell this information.

How we use it

How we share it

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate, such as Airtable (pitch form), GitHub Pages (website hosting), Google (fonts and email) and our email newsletter provider, and only as needed for them to provide their services. Our site links to portfolio company websites, LinkedIn and other sites. Their privacy practices are their own, and this policy does not cover them.

Data retention and security

We keep information for as long as it is useful for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices
